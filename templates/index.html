How it works

1

Search your route

We check every normal ticket from your city to your destination.

2

Look beyond it

We also search a few cities further on, and keep tickets that stop at your destination first.

3

Compare

If a longer ticket costs less, you could get off at the stopover. We show both, side by side.

Prices come from Google Flights via SerpApi and are a snapshot. This is a research project: we don't sell tickets. "View deal" opens the search on Google Flights.
