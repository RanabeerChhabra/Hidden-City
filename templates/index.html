Hidden City

Sometimes the longer flight is cheaper.

A ticket from Delhi to Goa with a stop in Mumbai can cost less than a ticket from Delhi to Mumbai. You can book the longer one and leave the airport at the stop. This looks for those fares on flights inside India.

      One way, one adult, economy.

      If you use a longer ticket: cabin bag only, because checked bags go to the final city. Book it one way, because airlines cancel the rest of a booking after a missed flight. Airlines don't allow this and can penalise frequent-flyer accounts.

      What I found

      In October 2026 I searched fares from Delhi to 41 cities on 5 dates. On 19 of 195 routes, a longer ticket beat the cheapest direct flight to the city it stops in. The best saving on those routes averaged ₹676, and the largest was ₹1,816.

      Jammu had one on all 5 dates, mostly on flights that carry on to Srinagar or Leh. So these fares are real, but they aren't common.

      How it works

      A search shows normal fares first. "Check longer tickets" then searches a few cities past your destination and keeps the tickets that stop there first. Prices come from Google Flights through SerpApi and can change by the time you book. Nothing is sold here: the links open the same search on Google Flights.
